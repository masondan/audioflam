When we started this initiative six years ago, we had no idea where we were going to end up. We just wanted to collect 2,000 books, send them over to my Verizon and walk away. But in four months, we had 10,000 books. Our little apartment was full of books. Overflowing my books. Today we have 10 libraries. By the end of this month, we would have built three more libraries. But these are not just ordinary buildings. These are places where kids can discover the joy of going on adventures with the Secret Seven.
